# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Set by the user: React + TypeScript, animated with GSAP, hosted on Vercel by the user himself. Delegated: Vite as the build tool. Local only until he deploys.

## Users

Inferred from the «همکاری» highlight, which sends people here: Iranian small-business owners who run their shop or service from Instagram, arriving on a phone, usually inside Instagram's in-app browser, after tapping the link sticker. They are busy and want to know what he does, see samples, and learn how working together goes.

Second audience: other developers, editors and designers who follow the page, looking at how the site is made.

## Product Purpose

The personal site of Kamyar Kazemi (@kamyar__kazemi). It holds what the highlight promises: «همه‌ی جزئیات، نمونه‌ها و نحوه‌ی همکاری، توی سایت». Success is a visitor who understands the offer, believes the work, and gets in touch, and who remembers the site itself as proof of the craft.

## Positioning

The site is made by the person it sells. The same hands edit the reel, animate the graphics, draw the sticker and write the React. The site has to demonstrate that rather than state it.

## Operating Context

Reached from Instagram: profile link, highlight link sticker, reels. Mostly phones, often on slow or filtered connections in Iran.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services: ادیت ریلز و ویدیو، موشن گرافیک و کاور، برنامه محتوا و کپشن و استوری و انتشار منظم، سایت و اپ، اتوماسیون با n8n.

Terms from his own copy: priced per project, no monthly contract, 50% at the start and 50% at delivery, 2 revisions included, direct messages stay with the client.

The site is fully bilingual, Persian and English, with a language switch. Persian is the default, right to left, with Persian digits.

A blog will be added later for SEO, so the build must be able to pre-render pages. It is not the current focus.

Open: real prices, contact method, final domain, how many samples he wants shown.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Kamyar Kazemi, «کامیار», handle @kamyar__kazemi. He presents himself as gamer, editor, front-end developer and motion designer.

The look of his page is binding: concrete paper, ruled grid, ink, one red, sticker lettering, torn labels, rubber stamps, tape, engraved cut-outs on a red block, the red dashed route. No teal, orange, gradients, glow, stock icons or emoji.

Voice: deadpan, short, warm, never cringe, never a skill show-off. Introductions open like «سلام. این کامیاره…». No blame. The word «کدنویس» is banned.

Never name past employers, clients or production projects unless he approves each one. Never use a photo of him on covers without asking.

## Evidence on Hand

Real: the intro reel and cover (video-pipeline/video-projects/kamyar-intro), song and lyric reels in video-pipeline/video-projects, the «جعبه‌ابزار» grid (toolbox-grid), the portfolio posters (Desktop/portfolio-*), the self-promo posters (self-promo-poster), the Okala redesign concept (a concept, labelled as one), the engraved sticker library.

Absent, and not to be fabricated: client names, testimonials, follower or view claims, performance numbers, prices.

## Product Principles

1. Prove it, don't claim it: the page itself is the sample.
2. A busy shop owner on a phone gets the offer in seconds, whatever the show around it.
3. His words stay his. Nothing is invented about him.
4. Every piece of the page behaves like a physical thing.
5. Fast on a bad connection beats impressive on a good one.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reduced-motion must leave a complete, readable site. All text stays real, selectable text except decorative lettering, which keeps a text equivalent. Contrast meets AA.
